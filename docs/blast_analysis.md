# EGFR BLAST Analysis

## Objective
Identify homologous EGFR proteins using BLASTp.

## Database
NCBI non-redundant protein database (nr)

## Key Observations
- Human EGFR showed nearly complete sequence conservation with closely related mammalian species.
- High query coverage and low E-values indicate strong evolutionary conservation.
- Conserved regions likely correspond to functionally important domains, especially the kinase domain.

## Biological Interpretation
The strong conservation of EGFR across species suggests preservation of critical structural and signaling functions.
