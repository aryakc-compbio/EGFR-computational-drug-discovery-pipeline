# EGFR Multiple Sequence Alignment

## Objective
Compare EGFR homologs across mammalian species.

## Observations
- EGFR sequences showed extensive conservation across species.
- Conserved regions correspond to functionally important domains.
- Minimal insertions/deletions observed in kinase-domain regions.

## Biological Interpretation
Strong sequence conservation suggests preservation of structural and signaling functions important for receptor activity and drug targeting.
