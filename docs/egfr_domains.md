# EGFR Domain Analysis

## Protein
Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR)

## Important Features and Domains

### Extracellular Repeat Regions
- Residues 75–300
- Residues 390–600

Function:
Involved in ligand binding and receptor activation.

---

### Dimerization/Activation Region
- Residues 690–706

Function:
Important for receptor dimerization, phosphorylation, and activation.

---

### Protein Kinase Domain
- Residues 714–981

Function:
Catalyzes phosphorylation and intracellular signaling pathways.

Biological Importance:
This domain is the primary target for EGFR inhibitors used in anticancer therapy and will be important for docking and molecular dynamics studies.

---

## Biological Relevance

EGFR is a receptor tyrosine kinase involved in cell proliferation and signaling. Dysregulation and mutations in the kinase domain are associated with multiple cancers, especially non-small cell lung cancer.
